\section*{Model}
\[
T = b_0 + b_1 k + b_2 N + b_3 kN + b_4 k^2 + b_5 N^2
\]

Coefficients (fitted to the table in \texttt{inference\_time\_data.png}):
\begin{align*}
b_0 &= 0.05348109 \\
b_1 &= 0.00532143 \\
b_2 &= 0.00173004 \\
b_3 &= 0.00803571 \\
b_4 &= -0.00446429 \\
b_5 &= -0.00092752 \\
R^2 &\approx 0.9888
\end{align*}
